# The-Gatekeepers Mission
The Gatekeeper is designed to restore integrity and trust to human skill development in the age of generative AI. It is not intended to block people out of ethical AI usage; rather, it is a human-in-the-loop validation system that encourages real-world mastery before granting unrestricted access to automated tools.

## Core Ethos
> "If you're not qualified to do things without AI, you are not qualified to do them with AI."

## Progression & Skill Unlocks
AI tools and advanced capabilities are locked behind experience gates that users open naturally through real-life learning and achievement:

1. **Beginner (High School / Novice):**
   - High guardrails and proactive safety warnings.
   - High risk-score sensitivity. AI encourages fundamental human study and directs users to human experts when risk thresholds are crossed.
2. **Intermediate (College / Dedicated Hobbyist):**
   - Moderate guardrails.
   - Example: Fan fiction or informal practice grants Intermediate status, allowing guided AI collaboration without full autonomous capabilities.
3. **Advanced (Master's / Doctorate / Senior Specialist):**
   - Low guardrails. High-context technical and conceptual support.
4. **Professional (Industry Practitioner / Monetized Skill):**
   - **Zero domain-specific guardrails.** Reserved for individuals actively making a living or publishing unassisted work in the field.
   - Example (Authors): Must write a full novel without AI assistance before using AI for professional writing workflows.

## The Vetting & Peer Endorsement Model
To reach Professional status in a domain, a user must satisfy two criteria:
1. **Proof of Unassisted Competency:** Verifiable human work (e.g., a published book created without AI, original portfolio, academic degree).
2. **Peer Vouching:** Endorsement by at least one verified Expert/Professional within the domain (inspired by ArXiv endorsement models).

Any user's experience tier can be challenged/flagged by peers, triggering elevated guardrails on flagged domains until proof is re-verified.
